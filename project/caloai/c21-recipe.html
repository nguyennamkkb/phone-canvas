Recipe
Creamy salmon pasta
25 min · Medium · 620 kcal per serving
High-protein Plans pick
Servings
2
Protein 41 g Carbs 58 g Fat 22 g
Ingredients · 6
Salmon fillet · 300 g
Whole-grain penne · 160 g
Light cream · 100 ml
Spinach · 60 g
Garlic · 2 cloves
Lemon · ½, juiced
No cream? Swap in Greek yogurt — saves ~90 kcal per serving.
Steps · 4
1 · Boil penne 9 min, reserve a cup of water.
2 · Sear salmon 3 min per side, flake roughly.
3 · Soften garlic + spinach, stir in cream + lemon.
4 · Toss pasta through sauce, top with salmon.
